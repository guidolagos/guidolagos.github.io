# About me
I'm an Assistant Professor at the [Faculty of Engineering of Sciences of Universidad Adolfo Ibanez (UAI)](https://ingenieria.uai.cl/assets/uploads/2020/04/plan-fic.pdf) at the [Vina del Mar campus](https://goo.gl/maps/3ELiL7XssZWZfXxt9). My research interests are in Applied Probability for Reliability Modeling, Stochastic Programming, and Computational Probability. I really enjoy programming and playing with computers and gadgets.

Previously, from Mar 2018 to Mar 2021, I was a FONDECYT postdoctoral researcher at [the Operations Research group at UAI](http://mansci-web.uai.cl/).
Also, from Oct 2018 to Apr 2020, I was an Assistant Researcher at the Industrial Engineering Department of the University of Santiago (USACH), and during all of 2017 I was a postdoc at the [Center for Mathematical Modeling, University of Chile](http://www.cmm.uchile.cl/).

I have a PhD in Operations Research from [Georgia Tech](https://www.isye.gatech.edu/), Atlanta, GA, USA.
My advisor was prof. [Ton Dieker](http://www.columbia.edu/~ad3217/) and broadly speaking our work was about mathematical guarantees for efficiency and accuracy of algorithms that simulate stochastic processes and specific events of them.
During 2016 I was a visiting student/research staff at the [IEOR department, Columbia University](http://ieor.columbia.edu).
In the past I worked with Prof. [Alexander Shapiro](http://www2.isye.gatech.edu/~ashapiro/) in multi-stage stochastic programming.

I studied Mathematical Engineering (at the time, a Chilean equivalent to a USA Math B.Sc. + Applied Math M.Sc.) at the [Mathematical Engineering Dept.](http://www.dim.uchile.cl/) of [Universidad de Chile](http://www.uchile.cl/english), and also have a M.Sc. degree in Operations Management from [Industrial Engineering Dept.](http://www.dii.uchile.cl/), Universidad de Chile too.
